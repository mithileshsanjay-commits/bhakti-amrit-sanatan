Spiritual and traditional knowledge disclaimer for Bhakti Amrit Sanatan. Educational and cultural guidance context.

1. Spiritual and Traditional Purpose

The contents published on Bhakti Amrit Sanatan—including mantras, shlokas, stotrams, puja vidhis, vrata rules, and mythological stories—are shared purely for spiritual, religious, and cultural education in accordance with traditional Sanatan Dharma heritage.

2. Not Medical, Psychological, or Legal Advice

Any references to mental tranquility, physical well-being, or the subtle effects of mantra chanting represent ancient Vedic spiritual beliefs, yogic philosophy, and time-honored traditions. They must never be construed as, or substituted for, professional medical diagnosis, psychiatric therapy, or scientific healthcare treatment.

If you are experiencing medical or mental health issues, please seek prompt counsel from qualified healthcare practitioners.

3. Astrological and Panchang Timings

Panchang timings, muhurat calculations, and festival dates are calculated using traditional Hindu astronomical tables and regional almanacs. Slight variations may exist based on local sunrise timings and regional geographic coordinates.

4. Scriptural Variations

Sanatan Dharma encompasses diverse sampradayas, puranas, and regional customs. Where multiple interpretations exist in scripture (e.g., Shiva Purana, Vishnu Purana, Srimad Bhagavata), we endeavor to present widely accepted traditional viewpoints with humility and respect.
