About Us — हमारे बारे में

Learn about Bhakti Amrit Sanatan, our mission to preserve and illuminate the timeless wisdom of Sanatan Dharma for global seekers.

1. Our Sacred Mission

Bhakti Amrit Sanatan is a dedicated spiritual initiative created to present the authentic, pristine knowledge of Sanatan Dharma to modern seekers across the world. Our mission is to preserve Vedic philosophy, timeless mantras, accurate puja rituals, and uplifting devotional narratives in an uncluttered, dignified digital sanctuary.

2. What We Provide

  • 🔱 Mantras & Stotrams: Vedic chants, peace mantras, and deity prayers with accurate pronunciation guidance and spiritual significance.
  • 🪔 Puja Vidhi: Authentic, step-by-step guidance for daily worship, festival rituals, and traditional shodashopachara puja.
  • 📅 Vrat & Festivals: Comprehensive schedules, religious observance rules, and spiritual significance of Ekadashi, Navratri, Shivratri, and other auspicious days.
  • 🕉️ Bhagavad Gita Wisdom: Chapter-by-chapter summaries and practical life applications of Lord Krishna's divine teachings.
  • 📖 Dev Katha & Puranic Mysteries: Uplifting stories from the Ramayana, Mahabharata, and Puranas illuminating dharma, devotion, and cosmic truth.

3. Commitment to Integrity

We believe sacred knowledge should remain unpolluted by commercial noise or misleading sensationalism. Our editorial team prioritizes scriptural accuracy, reverence, and clarity above all else.
